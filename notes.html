Notes in this game

Explore the notes used in Print Check and the printing errors they show.

Series names identify a design and signature combination, not the day a note came off the press. When a source does not document a printing date or a legible series, the record says so.

Sourcing and image use

Playable notes use a public-domain Smithsonian scan or credited crops from a Creative Commons photograph of the ANA Money Museum error-note display. The complete source image remains available from each entry.

Heritage specimens are retained below as reference records only. Its one-image noncommercial article allowance supports an individual collection entry, but does not clearly authorize cropped interactive gameplay.

Currency reproductions remain subject to U.S. law and the Bureau of Engraving and Printing's currency image use guidance.
