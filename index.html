How to play

  1. Inspect the note. Zoom or switch sides when both sides are documented.
  2. Tap the image to place a marker, or choose a numbered region.
  3. Select Check. Incorrect guesses can be moved and tried again.
  4. A correct answer earns one point unless Reveal was used.

Keyboard: use Tab to reach controls. In numbered mode, press 1 through 9 to choose a region, then press Enter on Check.
