How to play

  1. Inspect the piece. Zoom and pan for a closer look.
  2. Tap the image to place a marker, or choose a numbered region.
  3. Select Check. Move the marker and try again as often as you like.
  4. Use Hint or Show error whenever you want help.

Keyboard: use Tab to reach the controls. In numbered mode, press 1 through 9 to choose a region.
