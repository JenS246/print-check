How to play

Tap the photograph where you see the error. A correct tap reveals the answer. Try again after an incorrect tap, use Show error, or select Next at any time.

Keyboard controls

Tab to the photograph, then press 1 through 9 to choose an area. Keys 1-3 select the top row, 4-6 the middle row, and 7-9 the bottom row. The answer is checked immediately.
