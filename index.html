How to play

  1. Inspect the note. Zoom and pan when you need a closer look.
  2. Tap the image to place a marker, or choose a numbered region.
  3. Select Check. You can move the marker and try again.
  4. A correct answer earns one point unless you use Reveal.

Keyboard: use Tab to reach controls. In numbered mode, press 1 through 9 to choose a region, then use Check.
