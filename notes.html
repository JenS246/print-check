Pieces in this game

Genuine specimens, visible errors, and source records.

For paper currency, a series name is not an actual printing date. For coins, a date or mint mark is listed only when it is visible or documented. Missing details are never guessed.

Candidates awaiting material

These documented specimens are research leads, not playable pieces. Each remains out until the missing source material and display rights are secured.

    Sourcing and image use

    Playable notes use public-domain scans or credited crops from a Creative Commons photograph of the ANA Money Museum error-note display. Playable coins use unaltered Creative Commons photographs whose file records identify the pictured error.

    One Heritage specimen, the Del Monte note, is playable because a separate public-domain scan is available. Other Heritage images remain reference-only because the auction terms do not clearly authorize cropped interactive gameplay.

    Currency reproductions remain subject to U.S. law and the Bureau of Engraving and Printing's currency image use guidance.

    The documented 1973-D straight-clipped Jefferson nickel remains a research lead because the ANA publication does not grant clear reuse permission for its photographs.
